# UAT protocol

Pilot design: five participants, within-subject and counterbalanced. Three
participants start with Chat→Form and two with Form→Chat. Use equivalent tasks:
simple transaction, missing field clarification, preview edit, and cancel
before confirmation.

Record completion, elapsed time, step count, corrections, critical integrity
errors, SEQ and the ten SUS items. Report median/IQR and qualitative findings;
do not claim statistical significance at `n=5`. Copy `results.template.csv` to
`results.csv`; the latter is ignored because participant notes may be
identifying.
