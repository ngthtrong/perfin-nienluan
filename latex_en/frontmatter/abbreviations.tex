\frontchapter{Conventions for Status and Results}

This report clearly distinguishes between implemented components, measured
results, and behavior that exists only at the design level. This convention
prevents expectations or smoke tests from being presented as evidence of
completed quality.

\begin{longtable}{@{}L{3.0cm}L{5.2cm}L{5.8cm}@{}}
  \caption{Confidence levels for claims in this report}\label{tab:report-status}\\
  \toprule
  \textbf{Label} & \textbf{Meaning} & \textbf{How it is used} \\
  \midrule
  \endfirsthead
  \toprule
  \textbf{Label} & \textbf{Meaning} & \textbf{How it is used} \\
  \midrule
  \endhead
  Implemented & A corresponding component exists in source code, migrations, or tests. & Proves existence; does not by itself prove quality. \\
  Measured & A reproducible command, timestamp, and run result exist. & May be reported as a result. \\
  Target & A desired threshold used as an acceptance criterion. & Must not be presented as an achieved result. \\
  Design target & Expected behavior after in-scope fixes are completed. & Must be tested before it becomes ``measured.'' \\
  Out of scope & Not an objective of the current project. & Mentioned only under limitations or future work. \\
  \bottomrule
\end{longtable}
