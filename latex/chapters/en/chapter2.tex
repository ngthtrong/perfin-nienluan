\section{Financial Data and Preprocessing}
\label{sec:fin-data}

\subsection{Relational Ledger and Data Integrity}

A financial transaction minimally contains an amount, type, date, description, category and wallet. Income increases a balance, expense decreases it, and a wallet transfer creates two opposite changes that must not be counted as income or expense. Multi-record changes belong in one database transaction so they either complete or roll back together.

A relational database provides foreign keys, decimal types, domain constraints and aggregate queries. Durable business data must remain separate from short-lived clarification, preview, cache and queue state. Analyses defined on a fixed calendar axis zero-fill inactive periods: trends use completed months, correlations use completed ISO weeks, and runway/anomaly preserve every calendar day. Partial current months/weeks are excluded from comparisons; dropping zero periods distorts slopes, burn rate and correlation.

\subsection{Normalization and Text Similarity}
\label{subsec:text-similarity}

Input is normalized for case, punctuation, whitespace, currency units and relative dates before schema mapping. For strings $a,b$, Levenshtein similarity \cite{levenshtein1966} is

\begin{equation}
  s_{edit}=1-\frac{D_{lev}(a,b)}{\max(|a|,|b|)}.
\end{equation}

Here $a$ and $b$ are the two normalized input strings, $D_{lev}(a,b)$ is the minimum number of single-character insertions, deletions or substitutions needed to transform $a$ into $b$, and $|a|$ and $|b|$ are their character lengths. The resulting score is unitless and lies between 0 and 1 for non-empty strings, with larger values indicating greater similarity.

For token sets $T_a,T_b$, the Dice coefficient \cite{dice1945} is

\begin{equation}
  s_{dice}=\frac{2|T_a\cap T_b|}{|T_a|+|T_b|}.
\end{equation}

Here $T_a$ and $T_b$ are the sets of normalized tokens from the two inputs, $|T_a|$ and $|T_b|$ are their cardinalities, and $|T_a\cap T_b|$ counts tokens shared by both sets. When the denominator is non-zero, the coefficient is unitless and ranges from 0 (no shared tokens) to 1 (identical token sets).

Levenshtein distance captures nearby typing errors, whereas Dice is useful when phrase order or components differ. PERFIN combines exact matches, aliases, both scores and a safety margin between the two best candidates. The chosen thresholds are detailed in Section~\ref{subsec:classification-design}.

\section{Explainable Analytical Techniques}
\label{sec:algorithms}

\subsection{Trend, Anomaly and Correlation}

Ordinary least squares (OLS) \cite{montgomery2012} models a time series as $\hat y_i=a+bx_i$. Its slope is

\begin{equation}
  b=\frac{\sum_i(x_i-\bar{x})(y_i-\bar{y})}
          {\sum_i(x_i-\bar{x})^2}.
\end{equation}

Here $i$ indexes the observations in the selected time window, $x_i$ is the time position of observation $i$, $y_i$ is its measured value, and $\bar{x}$ and $\bar{y}$ are the corresponding sample means. In $\hat y_i=a+bx_i$, $a$ is the intercept, $b$ is the fitted change per unit of $x$, and $\hat y_i$ is the fitted value. The slope has the unit of $y$ per unit of time, whereas $R^2$ is a unitless goodness-of-fit value between 0 and 1.

The sign of $b$ gives direction and $R^2$ gives linear fit. OLS is explainable and testable but does not model seasonality or future events. A z-score compares an observation with the mean in standard-deviation units, while the interquartile range is more robust to extremes \cite{tukey1977}. PERFIN uses both as review signals, not as fraud conclusions.

Pearson's coefficient \cite{pearson1895} measures linear co-movement:

\begin{equation}
  r=\frac{\sum_i(x_i-\bar{x})(y_i-\bar{y})}
  {\sqrt{\sum_i(x_i-\bar{x})^2\sum_i(y_i-\bar{y})^2}}.
\end{equation}

Here $x_i$ and $y_i$ are paired measurements from the same observation window, $i$ indexes the $n$ retained pairs, and $\bar{x}$ and $\bar{y}$ are their respective means. The denominator scales the joint variation by the two series' standard deviations, so $r$ is unitless and ranges from $-1$ (perfect inverse linear association) to $1$ (perfect positive linear association), with values near 0 indicating little linear association.

Correlation is interpreted only within the observed window and does not imply causation.
The coefficient is undefined with fewer than three paired observations or when either
series has zero variance; that case must be represented as a null value rather than
being reported as $r=0$. PERFIN also removes calendar weeks in which both categories
are zero and reports the effective paired support, preventing shared absence from
being mistaken for co-movement.

\subsection{Runway, Recurring Items, Budgets and Goals}

Runway extrapolates how long liquid balance in the reporting currency can support the recent spending rate; it is not total wealth. Recurring-item discovery groups transactions by description, amount and date interval. Budget forecasting uses spending pace over elapsed days, while goal planning uses the remaining amount, deadline, contribution and debt simulation. These techniques prioritize transparent assumptions and boundary handling. PERFIN's formulas and windows appear in Section~\ref{subsec:analytics-design}.

\section{OCR, STT and Language Models}

\subsection{OCR and Speech-to-Text}
\label{subsec:ocr-stt}

OCR generally includes image preprocessing, text-region detection, recognition and post-processing; PP-OCR is a representative compact OCR pipeline \cite{ppocr2020}. STT converts audio to a transcript; Whisper and Vietnamese PhoWhisper illustrate large-scale and language-adapted recognition \cite{radford2022,phowhisper2024}. PERFIN runs PaddleOCR and PhoWhisper locally on the backend host. Both services only return raw text and provider metadata; the result follows the same normalization, preview and confirmation pipeline as manual input. Extracted text may subsequently be sent to Gemini for language parsing only when that provider is configured.

Quality requires ground truth. CER/WER measure text error, while transaction-field accuracy measures the effect on amount, date, category and line items. A smoke test on a few files establishes provider execution, not accuracy.

\subsection{Local Parser and LLM}

Transformers use attention to model token relationships and underpin modern LLMs \cite{vaswani2017}. Multimodal models such as Gemini extend this ability to text and media \cite{gemini2023}. Structured output constrains shape but cannot prove that an amount is correct, a category belongs to the user or an operation is authorized.

PERFIN uses provider-schema function calling \cite{geminifunctioncalling}. The LLM proposes a tool and arguments; the backend validates, builds a preview and waits for confirmation. For insight narration, the LLM only receives precomputed facts. This preserves interaction benefits without making the model a source of truth.

\section{Related Applications and Selected Direction}

Money Lover \cite{moneylover} and MISA MoneyKeeper \cite{misamoneykeeper} represent form- and dashboard-oriented finance applications. They are strong at structured records but require many entry steps. Chatbots reduce entry work yet risk untraceable numbers; specialist analytics are often difficult for general users.

PERFIN does not attempt commercial feature completeness. It targets the intersection of a relational ledger, deterministic algorithms, human confirmation and a bounded language layer. This direction fits the project's data-and-algorithm focus and allows each part to be evaluated independently.
