The following full tables retain the same fields and identifiers as the
specification template. They are moved here only to keep the main chapter

\begin{frtable}{FR-06 --- Transfer Funds and Record Special Cash Flows}{tab:app-fr06}
  \textbf{Feature name} & Wallet transfer and investment gain/loss. \frtablerow
  \textbf{ID} & FR-06. \frtablerow
  \textbf{User} & User. \frtablerow
  \textbf{Necessity} & Desirable. \frtablerow
  \textbf{Classification} & Complex. \frtablerow
  \textbf{Participants and concerns} & One user action must update both wallets and history consistently. \frtablerow
  \textbf{Summary} & Record internal transfers without changing total assets and classify investment cash flow correctly. \frtablerow
  \textbf{Relationships} & Uses FR-05 wallet and transaction entities. \frtablerow
  \textbf{Normal flow} & Enter source, target, amount and date; validate distinct wallets; lock in stable order; debit, credit and write history; commit. \frtablerow
  \textbf{Subflows} & In one transaction, subtract source, add target and write \code{wallet_transfers}; net change equals zero. \frtablerow
  \textbf{Alternate flows} & Same, missing or out-of-scope wallets, invalid amount/date, or different wallet currencies roll back. A future negative source balance alone is not rejected. \frtablerow
\end{frtable}

\begin{frtable}{FR-11 --- Manage Recurring Bills and Proactive Jobs}{tab:app-fr11}
  \textbf{Feature name} & Recurring bills, payments and proactive messages. \frtablerow
  \textbf{ID} & FR-11. \frtablerow
  \textbf{User} & User; Background worker. \frtablerow
  \textbf{Necessity} & Desirable. \frtablerow
  \textbf{Classification} & Complex. \frtablerow
  \textbf{Participants and concerns} & The user needs correct due dates; the worker needs retry and idempotency; PostgreSQL must prevent duplicate effects. \frtablerow
  \textbf{Summary} & Manage schedules, record one payment per period and generate grounded reminders/alerts. \frtablerow
  \textbf{Relationships} & Reads FR-05/FR-07; may call FR-08 for wording only. \frtablerow
  \textbf{Normal flow} & Scheduler enqueues a user-scoped job; worker loads due data; computes deterministic facts; persists an event by unique key; advances schedule after success. \frtablerow
  \textbf{Subflows} & Payment writes payment history, transaction and next due date atomically. \frtablerow
  \textbf{Alternate flows} & Duplicate event/job returns the existing result. Transient failure retries with backoff. Redis failure stops the worker while the interactive API may continue. \frtablerow
\end{frtable}
